# Sponsorship Proposal — Custom Harley Benton R-457MN WH MultiScale + EU Christmas Giveaway

## Project

Seeking sponsors for the customization of a **Harley Benton R-457MN WH MultiScale** guitar, combined with a **giveaway for EU followers at Christmas**.

## Objectives

- Boost the visibility of partner brands and of the channel.
- Showcase products through a **DIY mini-series of 3–4 episodes** covering a home-made guitar upgrade.
- Generate qualified subscribers, views, engagement, and additional sales via affiliate or direct links.
- Highlight the partner brand and the quality of its products.
- Keep the project at **break-even**, with a realistic win-win outcome for everyone:
  - **The community**: educational guitar-modding videos + a giveaway.
  - **The sponsors**: product exposure, qualified followers, and potential additional sales.
  - **Myself**: visibility, authority, and qualified subscribers.
- Increase visibility with a **European audience**.

**Risk/benefit ratio: the benefits clearly outweigh the risks for everyone involved.**

## Preliminary Study

Before launching, I will measure the project's reach and potential, and set clear conditions to avoid any flop or unnecessary spending, using **one carousel + one teaser reel**.

Both posts will run on **Instagram and Facebook**:

- **An introduction carousel** presenting the Christmas giveaway, introducing you as the first sponsor (making it easier to approach other partners), and collecting donations to reach break-even.
- **A teaser reel** to push the project through Reels/Shorts and drive audience discovery.

On other platforms: **one reel on TikTok/YouTube**.

### Two possible outcomes

1. **Momentum is there, partners join, and the balance is ≥ 0**: the giveaway is officially launched with a carousel (the contest entry point, presenting the participation rules, the sponsors, the end date, etc.).
2. **Momentum is lacking / the balance is unfavorable**: I will still produce the mini-series the same way (with a more limited budget), without the giveaway, relying on its organic reach. Keep in mind that 7-string modding content is quite uncommon, so it can still achieve good visibility and solid returns for you through affiliate links.

## What's in It for You

- **Low risk on your side**: guaranteed visibility within a qualified niche of **7.2K followers** — guitarists of all levels, professionals, and luthiers from all over the world. You break even from **a single sale** linked to the video.
- **A dedicated product video** (videos usually run 1:00–1:30). In your case, it would cover the **unboxing, presentation, and installation of the Ratio Tuners** on the guitar.
- The dedicated video will include the appropriate product link in my Linktree — ideally an **affiliate link**. Even better: tagging the product directly on Instagram (requires an Instagram Shop on your side).
- To drive conversions, we can pair this with a **promo code** — a pinned comment with a visible promo code is effective, costs nothing, and encourages people to save/share the videos to come back later and buy the product.
- Through **Instagram Collab posts**, you can display the posts on your own profile and reuse my content for your social networks and ad campaigns.
- You will be **tagged as much as possible** and invited to collaborate on every post: you choose which posts you want to highlight and how visible you want to be.

## Target / Entry Point / Conditions

- The giveaway will only be valid **on Instagram, under the official post**, but will have entry points across all networks. The goal is to maximize visibility and attract qualified followers primarily on Instagram, and secondarily on your other social networks.
- For simplicity's sake, the giveaway is **restricted to EU residents** (simplified shipping).

### Participation conditions

- Reside in the EU (simplified delivery).
- Follow the sponsors and my channel.
- On the entry-point post: share, repost, save — and comment by tagging 2 people + "Merry Christmas".
- Interact with the mini-series posts (optional, but it drives engagement).

**Participation is free and open to everyone.** The giveaway closes on **December 24** with a random draw. The winner will be contacted directly by me. If they do not reply within **72 hours**, if their postal address/phone number is not in the EU, or if any condition is not met, the guitar will be put back into play.

## Shipping

The guitar will be shipped with the neck unbolted, protected and packed in a cardboard box, handled by **UPS** — most likely during **January**, after the holidays.

## Strategy

- For maximum traction within the first **48 hours**: a Collab post on your feed + active commenting, sharing, and reposting on your side, on every social network, with the official post link, as soon as it goes live (shares/saves are the main driver of the algorithm). This secures reach and optimizes the launch.
- In terms of volume, a reasonable plan would be **3–4 videos + 2 carousels** for this mini-series, spread from **November to December**, at a pace of roughly **one post per week** 👍

If you are interested in inviting collaborators of any size for the remaining equipment, I am completely open to it — you were my first choice. For example:

- Pickups, mono-rail bridge, multiscale nut, various hardware, strap, gig bag, picks, tuner, cleaning products, jack, etc.

Note that I have a set of **Fishman Fluence Tosin Abasi** pickups in reserve as a backup in case no pickup sponsor joins but donations come in. I would also like to fit the guitar with a **LiPo battery mod with a USB-C port** to recharge the pickups without a 9V battery.

The idea is to keep things simple: the mods need to remain accessible DIY projects that people can do at home — if it's plug-and-play, even better.

The dream upgrade would result in a finished guitar worth around **€600–700** for a very limited cost, with potentially great returns: product visibility, possible additional sales for every participant — and a very merry Christmas for the winner. 🎄🎸
